# Comprehensive Technical Analysis Report: INFY.NS

INFY.NS (Infosys Limited) has exhibited a remarkable recovery over the past month, surging approximately **15.5%** from ₹1,000.4 to ₹1,155.6 (as of July 29, 2026). This analysis examines the confluence of multiple technical indicators to assess whether this rally has sustainable momentum or if caution is warranted.

## Detailed Trend Analysis

### Recovery Pattern
INFY.NS experienced a significant recovery beginning in early July after testing support around ₹982.4 on July 1. The stock has since climbed steadily, with particularly strong moves on July 24 (₹1,040.9) and July 29 (₹1,155.6).

### Confluence of Bullish Signals
1. **Moving Average Convergence**: The 10 EMA has crossed above the 50 SMA, and the price is now above both.
2. **MACD Momentum**: Positive histogram values indicate accelerating upward momentum.
3. **RSI Strength**: Climbing from oversold to neutral territory confirms improving sentiment.
4. **Volume Confirmation**: VWMA confirms that buying volume supports the price action.

### Resistance and Support Levels
- **Immediate Resistance**: ₹1,164.5 (recent high from July 29)
- **Key Support**: ₹1,088.4 (VWMA/10 EMA area)
- **Stronger Support**: ₹1,040.6 (50 SMA)

## Risk Assessment

While the technical picture is bullish, several cautionary factors exist:
1. The stock remains well below its 200-day SMA, indicating the long-term downtrend may not be fully reversed.
2. Breaking above the upper Bollinger Band may lead to profit-taking and a pullback.
3. High volatility (ATR) suggests potential for sharp reversals.
