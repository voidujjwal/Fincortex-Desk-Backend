## 5️⃣  Past Mistakes & Lessons Learned (Self‑Reflection)  

| Mistake | What Happened | Lesson Applied Today |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| **Over‑relying on a single momentum indicator** (2022) | Sold a small position when MACD turned slightly negative for a day, missing a 40 % rally that followed. | **Now**: I treat MACD as *one* piece of a multi‑indicator checklist; I require a *confluence* (price, volume, MACD cross) before acting. |
| **Panic‑selling on a 3‑day dip below the 200‑SMA** (2023) | Cut losses at ~ 12 % loss when price briefly slipped below 200‑SMA, only to watch a rapid rebound and 30 % upside. | **Now**: I verify *at least two confirming signals* (e.g., volume surge + positive MACD crossover) before labeling a dip a “breakdown.” |
| **Ignoring volume on a bounce off Bollinger band** (early 2024) | Missed a “dead‑cat‑bounce” that later turned into a strong rally because I assumed low volume meant no conviction. | **Now**: I **scan the volume profile**; a bounce with < average volume is dismissed as a false signal. |
| **Chasing high‑valuation “growth” stocks without checking forward fundamentals** (2025) | Added to a position in a high‑growth AI‑chip maker based on headline growth, only to see earnings revisions stall and price collapse 22 %. | **Now**: I always overlay *forward fundamentals* (PEG, EPS revisions) onto valuation before committing capital. |
| **Failing to set a hard stop‑loss when entering on a “buy‑the‑dip” premise** (mid‑2025) | Entered at $158, expecting a bounce, but price kept sliding to $150 before I could exit; loss of 5 %. | **Now**: I **pre‑define a stop‑loss** (typically 3‑5 % below entry or just below key technical support) and enforce it strictly. |

**Takeaway for today:** The market is presenting a *perfect storm* of bearish technicals (MACD histogram widening, RSI decay, price under 200‑SMA) accompanied by softening fundamentals and emerging competition. My past mistakes taught me to *wait for confirmation* before assuming a bounce, and to *respect risk controls* above all else.
